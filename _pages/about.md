I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am advised by **Professor Junxian He**. He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
